Privacy policy

Sunsy collects no data about you. This page explains what stays on your phone and the few things that go to Apple.

Effective 19 September 2026. Applies to the iPhone app Sunsy: Jet Lag Planner (“Sunsy”, “the app”).

In short
  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • The developer collects no data. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.
  • What you enter stays on your iPhone. Delete it any time.

Who we are

Sunsy is made by Marcus Domann, who is responsible for this policy and for the app. Contact: tmin@marcado.io.

What stays on your iPhone

Sunsy stores the following only on your device, so it can compute your times:

  • your usual wake time and bedtime,
  • your trips (destination, arrival time and the plan Sunsy works out),
  • your settings, such as reminders and the sky setting,
  • which windows you marked as done.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no server. Your iPhone may include this data in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Location

If you allow it, Sunsy uses your location only while you are using the app (“When In Use”). It uses it for two things:

  • Sunrise and sunset. Sunsy computes today’s sun times on your phone from your location. This involves no network request.
  • Local weather. Sunsy asks Apple’s weather service (WeatherKit) for the current weather. To do that, the app sends your approximate coordinates to Apple. Apple does not share this request with the developer. Apple’s handling is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Sunsy does not use location in the background and does not keep a location history. If you decline location access, you can pick your city by hand instead.

City search

When you plan a trip or pick a city, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps (MapKit) to find matching places. Apple processes it under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer never sees your searches.

Photos and camera

The share feature lets you put your sunrise or sunset times on a photo.

  • Photos. Sunsy uses the iOS system photo picker. It does not ask for access to your photo library, and it only receives the one photo you choose.
  • Camera. Sunsy asks for the camera only when you tap “Take a photo”.
  • Composing. The image is put together on your phone. It leaves your phone only when you share it yourself, through the iOS share sheet or Instagram Stories, to the app or person you choose. That app’s own privacy policy then applies.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules and shows itself. No push service and no server are involved. You can turn them off in Sunsy’s Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

What we do not do

Sunsy has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites, does not sell or share data, and does not build a profile of you. The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data through the app.

If you email support, your message and email address are used only to reply to you and are not used for anything else.

Deleting your data

Open Sunsy, go to Settings and tap Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Sunsy is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to complain to a data protection authority. Since the developer holds no personal data from the app, these requests will normally have nothing to act on, but you are welcome to ask at tmin@marcado.io.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Sunsy ever starts collecting data, the app will say so before it happens.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
